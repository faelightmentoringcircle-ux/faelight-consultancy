FAELIGHT LOGO — drop your logo files in this folder (public/brand/).

Once the files are here, tell Claude the filenames and they'll be wired into
the header, footer, hero, about page, brochures and favicon.

MOST USEFUL VERSIONS (PNG or SVG, SVG preferred if you have it):

1. logo-mark.(png/svg)      — JUST the winged-lantern emblem, on a TRANSPARENT
                              background. Used in the header + as the small mark.
                              Transparent is important so it sits cleanly on the
                              dark green/purple hero and footer.

2. logo-full.(png/svg)      — emblem + "Faelight" wordmark stacked, TRANSPARENT
                              background. Used large on the hero / about / brochure cover.

3. logo-wordmark.(png/svg)  — (optional) just the "Faelight" wordmark text, transparent.

SUB-BRAND LOGOS (each sub-brand has its own emblem):

4. mentoring-circle.(png/svg)  — the winged "M" Mentoring Circle emblem, transparent.
                                 Used on the /mentoring page + its brochure.
5. systems.(png/svg)           — the Systems sub-brand emblem, transparent.
                                 Used on the /systems page + its brochure.
6. experiences.(png/svg)       — the Experiences sub-brand emblem, transparent.
                                 Used on the /experiences page + its brochure.

If you only have combined images on the cream background, that's fine too
— send them and Claude will work with them (and can note where a transparent
cut-out would look better on dark sections).

Tip: for crisp scaling everywhere, an SVG export is ideal. Otherwise PNG at ~2x
the display size (e.g. mark ~200px tall, full ~600px tall) with transparency.
